Things to Check Before You Play

  • Platform: Does the game work on your PC, console or phone?
  • Cost: Is it free or paid? Free games may sell optional items inside the game.
  • Age rating: Is the content suitable for your age?
  • Players: Can you play alone, or do you need other people and internet?
  • Device power: Will your device run it smoothly?
  • Time: Do you have enough time to play it?
